The Shape of Things to Come

Autumn 2026

If You Had Wings · Angharad Matthews. Photo: Jamie Panton.

Lighting, sound and video design for a season of new performances, each shaped through collaboration with a different artist.

Year2026
FormTheatre
RoleLighting, sound & video design
ContextVolcano Theatre, Abertawe

Autumn 2026 · Volcano Theatre

Across the autumn season at Volcano, I designed lighting, sound and video for a changing programme of new performance. Each collaboration called for a different relationship between the performer, the room, the image and the sound.

My work brought the three design disciplines together, responding to each artist’s language and the practical demands of presenting new work week by week.

Window Cleaner of the Apocalypse

Tiago Gambogi24–26 September 2026

After the end of the world, a migrant cleaner continues tending the gallery windows. Cleaning tools, samba and stubborn optimism become a way of caring for beauty amid devastation, bringing an ordinary working routine into an extraordinary future.

Tocyn Iaith

Rufus Mufasa1–3 October 2026

A bilingual performance set in a future Wales where institutions control who can use Welsh. A valid token is required to speak it, placing the language within a bureaucratic system and raising questions about its ownership by communities.

Monday Morning at Machine Mart

Sahana Athreya15–17 October 2026

Inspired by an encounter in a Cardiff gadget shop, the performance follows everyday life on the high street. Exchanges around inexpensive consumer goods become a way to examine the relationships between migration, the economy and a changing climate.

The Last Place Left

Georgia Griffiths22–24 October 2026

In a near future where friendship is sold through subscriptions and attention has become a commodity, a small group enters an underground archive. It holds traces of human connection as the communities outside it begin to disappear.

If You Had Wings

Angharad Matthews29–31 October 2026

Inspired by Wim Wenders’s Wings of Desire, an angel returns to a future high street devastated by war. Once a listener and comforter to its residents, she searches for human connection and the possibility of helping to build a more peaceful future.
