Ble Mae Fy Nghartref

Sound, lighting and audiovisual design for Eric Ngalle Charles and Volcano’s collaboration.

Year2026
FormTheatre
RoleSound, lighting & AV design
ContextVolcano Theatre

Ble Mae Fy Nghartref (Where is My Home?) brings Eric Ngalle Charles’s lived experience into a collaboration with Volcano, adapted and directed by Paul Davies. Two racist incidents become the starting point for exploring identity, belonging and the meaning of home through stories and memories. Developed with Arts Council of Wales support in April 2026, the project was shared with audiences in Swansea through two work-in-progress performances on 24 and 25 April.
