what the fuck is going on? (or how I see things)

An authored solo performance bringing together projection, sound, satire and research into artificial intelligence and its social implications.

Year2025
FormPerformance
RoleWriter, performer, composer & video designer
ContextVolcano Theatre, Abertawe

Commissioned by Volcano · The Shape of Things to Come · 29–31 May 2025

I wrote, performed, scored and designed this solo work for Volcano Theatre. Projection, music, live performance and satire formed a response to artificial intelligence and its social implications, approached from a working-class perspective.

The commission brought writing, performance, composition and visual design into one work. It was presented as part of The Shape of Things to Come in May 2025.
