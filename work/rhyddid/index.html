Rhyddid

An interactive installation connecting a suspended wooden map of Wales with projected histories, original music and audience participation.

Year2021
FormInstallation
RoleArtist
ContextGuildhall School of Music & Drama, London

2021 · Interactive projection installation · Guildhall School of Music & Drama, London

An interactive projection installation combining a suspended wooden map of Wales with fragments of Welsh history. Audience interaction influenced the material encountered, bringing physical structure, projection and participation into a shared spatial experience.
