Y Cynfod

A gathering of faceless clay figures, given shifting presence by projected light and sound. Y Cynfod follows the impulse to make, from clay and fire to paint and code, and asks what it means for matter to become aware of itself.

Year2026
FormSculpture, painting, moving image & writing
RoleArtist
ContextElysium College Street Gallery, Abertawe

Shades of Existence · 26 June–5 July 2026 · Curated by Hannah Jones

Y Cynfod, the before-beings, are faceless clay figures poised at the threshold of becoming a person. Their uneven bodies retain the marks of making; their lack of features leaves identity open. They give a human shape to a question that runs through the project: how does matter come to recognise itself?

Developed for Shades of Existence at Elysium Gallery, the work brings clay, projection, moving image, sound, painting and storytelling into a shared world. In We Were Here, projected colour finds the figures and moves on, changing their visibility without removing their presence. The CRT video smallfires imagines their origin through fire, light and sound. Paintings and a short story carry that world beyond the installation.

At its centre is a continuity between old and new ways of making. Shaping clay, tending fire, painting a figure and writing code all begin with the desire to give form to something felt. The project approaches existence through warmth, attention and the traces people leave: an invitation to spend time with these small presences, and to recognise something of our own unfinishedness in them.
