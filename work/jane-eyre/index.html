Jane Eyre: A Musical

Associate video design and programming for the musical at Southwark Playhouse Elephant.

Year2026
FormTheatre
RoleAssociate video design & programming
ContextSouthwark Playhouse Elephant

28 August–24 October 2026 · Programme credit: Video Associate / Programmer

Charlotte Brontë’s novel becomes a musical about love, independence and the consequences of concealed truths. Jane arrives at Thornfield Hall as a governess and forms an attachment to Edward Rochester, but what she discovers there challenges the future she wants for herself. Written by John Caird and Paul Gordon, this UK premiere was co-directed by Caird and Megan McGinnis at Southwark Playhouse Elephant.
