Studies for a Loving Machine

An ongoing body of work exploring how energy, data, infrastructure and other systems become part of everyday experience.

Year2025–ongoing
FormInstallation
RoleArtist

Moving image · Installation · Sound · Ongoing

Studies for a Loving Machine explores the systems that organise everyday life and how they are experienced through particular places. Moving image, sound, objects and live data offer different ways to encounter processes that are often difficult to see.

5th World Congress of Psychogeography

In September 2026, I presented work from the project and gave a talk at the 5th World Congress of Psychogeography in Swansea. The installation brought together a CRT television showing a live feed of Kilvey Hill, a composited layer of abstracted geospatial data, and sail-like forms moving in response to live weather data.

Grid Pulse

A browser-based sound instrument developed as part of the wider project. Grid Pulse uses electrical grid frequency, regional carbon-intensity data and the location of electrical infrastructure as material for sound.
